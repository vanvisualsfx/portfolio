About

Story first. Then the polish.

You get one person for the whole job. I plan it, shoot it, edit it, grade it, and subtitle it, so nothing gets lost between a crew and an editor. Already have footage? I start from the edit.

My rule is story first. I find the moment people will stay for, build the cut around it, then make it look and sound like it cost more than it did.

I'm Van Santos, a Filipino filmmaker in Salamanca, Spain. I shoot on a Sony A7 V, finish in DaVinci Resolve Studio, and work in English and Filipino, Taglish included. Short-form comes back in 1–2 days, with two revision rounds included.
